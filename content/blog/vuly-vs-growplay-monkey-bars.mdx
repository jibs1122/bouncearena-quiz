Vuly and Growplay are two Australian brands selling freestanding monkey bars. They differ in height range, warranty, what's included and price.

## The two brands

### Growplay Monkey Bars

Growplay is an Australian-owned and operated company based in Perth, where it designs and engineers all its products in-house.

Growplay says its frames are <a href="https://growplaymonkeybars.com/collections/backyard-monkey-bars" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">load tested to 950 kg and certified to Australian, USA and European standards</a>. Base models include several play features as standard rather than as add-ons.

Its freestanding, height-adjustable monkey bars come in a "Bubble Gum Blue" colour and have a <a href="https://growplaymonkeybars.com/pages/warranty" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">10-year warranty</a>.

### Vuly Quest Monkey Bars

Vuly is an Australian company best known for its trampolines. It also sells swing sets and monkey bars, and its Quest 2 range comes in standard and Pro models.

Vuly focuses on features such as integrated shade roofs and, on the Pro models, elevated hideouts. Its freestanding monkey bars meet the Australian Standard AS 8124, use Vuly's joiner technology and have a 7-year warranty.

Vuly's [Monkey Bar Configurator](https://www.vulyplay.com/aff/100/?url=monkey-bars/configurator) lets you try different sizes and add-ons to see what will fit in your backyard.


## Design and construction

Both brands offer freestanding designs made from galvanised and powder-coated steel tubing, but their specifications differ:

**Height range**

- **Growplay:** Adjustable from 1.5 m to 2.3 m
- **Vuly:** Adjustable from 1.2 m to 1.95 m

**Warranty**

- **Growplay:** 10-year warranty on frames
- **Vuly:** 7-year warranty on frames, 2-year warranty on swings, 1-year on accessories

**Load testing**

- **Growplay:** Load tested to 950 kg, according to Growplay
- **Vuly:** Specific load rating not advertised

**Standard inclusions**

- **Growplay:** Multiple accessories included as standard with base models
- **Vuly:** Shade roof included standard with all models

**Construction features**

- **Growplay:** Patented ground anchors for stability; signature "Bubble Gum Blue" colour
- **Vuly:** X-Brace™ frame system in Pro models; joiner technology

## Model range

### Growplay models

- **Classic Medium Frame**: Entry-level model for basic monkey bar play
- **The Compact**: Designed for small backyards and narrow spaces
- **Adventurer Large Frame**: Larger model with extensive play area
- **Explorer XL Frame**: Largest model for maximum play area

### Vuly Quest 2 models

- **Small Quest 2**: Compact design
- **Medium Quest 2**: Mid-sized option
- **Large Quest 2**: Expanded play area
- **Large Ninja Quest 2**: Specialised model with battle bars for ninja training
- **Extra Large Quest 2**: Maximum play area with dual monkey bar sections

**Pro Variants**: All Vuly models (except Extra Large) are available in Pro versions that include:

- Elevated hideout play platform
- X-Brace stability system
- Cargo net access
- Increased total height (308 cm vs 264 cm standard height)

**Key differences in model range:**

- Growplay offers a more affordable entry point with their Classic model
- Vuly models all come with shade covers included
- Vuly offers more specialised variants (Pro and Ninja models)

## Distinctive features

### Vuly Quest 2

- **Sun protection:** Shade roof standard on all models
- **X-Brace system:** Extra bracing for stability in Pro models
- **Battle bars:** Ninja models add obstacle-course training equipment
- **Half Price Parts For Life:** Ongoing discount program for registered products
- **Attachment options:** Up to 15 attachment points on Extra Large model
- **Free basketball set:** Included with all models at time of writing

- **Higher load rating:** Growplay says its frames are load tested to 950 kg
- **Accessory bundle:** Many accessories included as standard rather than as add-ons
- **Longer warranty:** 10-year frame warranty (vs 7-year)
- **Colour:** "Bubble Gum Blue" finish

## Which brand suits your family

### Growplay is the better fit if:

**Your priority is value and included accessories**

- More accessories included as standard with base models
- Lower entry price with the Classic model (about $1,000)
- Potentially better long-term value with the 10-year warranty

**You have older or taller children**

- Higher maximum height adjustment (2.3 m vs 1.95 m)
- More headroom for growing children or adult use

**You want a published load rating**

- Growplay says its frames are load tested to 950 kg
- Especially relevant if adults plan to use the equipment too

### Vuly Quest 2 is the better fit if:

**You want specialised features**

- Elevated section in Pro models creates multi-level play
- Shade roof included as standard on all models
- Ninja models with specialised obstacle training features

**You need flexible configuration options**

- Clear upgrade paths between standard and Pro models
- Extensive range of sizes from small to extra-large
- Ability to add up to 15 accessories on larger models
