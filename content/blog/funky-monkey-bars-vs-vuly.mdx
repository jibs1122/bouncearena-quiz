Funky Monkey Bars suits families who want the longer frame warranty (10 years against Vuly's 7), a taller top bar (2.3 m against 1.95 m) and Australian-made steel. The Vuly Quest 2 suits families who want a shade roof included, an elevated hideout on the Pro models or battle bars on the Ninja version.

Both are Australian brands of backyard monkey bars.

## Brand overview

**Funky Monkey Bars** is a family-owned business based in Perth, Western Australia. Its products are Australian-made, using Australian galvanised steel with Dulux outdoor powder coating. Its free-standing, height-adjustable monkey bars don't need concrete.

**Vuly** is an Australian company best known for its trampolines, and also sells swing sets and monkey bar systems. Its Quest 2 range has adjustable monkey bars with modular designs and joiner technology, and the Pro models add elevated hideouts.


## Design and construction

### Funky Monkey Bars

- Free-standing design with patented "twirly-whirly" bars for stability
- Height adjustable from 1.2 m to 2.3 m
- Australian galvanised steel with Dulux powder coating
- No concrete needed; pegs into grass
- 10-year warranty on frames
- Load ratings from 750 kg to 2,340 kg depending on model

### Vuly Quest 2

- Joiner technology for stability
- Height adjustable from 1.2 m to 1.95 m
- Double galvanisation and powder coating for rust resistance
- Pro models include [X-Brace™ frame system](https://www.vulyplay.com/aff/100/?url=monkey-bars/quest-2-pro/large) for additional stability
- 7-year warranty on frames
- Includes shade roof for sun protection

Vuly’s [Monkey Bar Configurator](https://www.vulyplay.com/aff/100/?url=monkey-bars/configurator) lets you try different sizes and add-ons to see what fits your backyard.

## Which brand is right for you?

**Funky Monkey Bars is the better fit if you:**

- Want a longer warranty (10 years vs 7 years)
- Need a taller height range (up to 2.3 m vs 1.95 m)
- Appreciate the Australian-made manufacturing
- Want the "twirly-whirly" bars for both play and stability

**Vuly Quest 2 is the better fit if you:**

- Want built-in features like the elevated hideout (Pro models)
- Prefer integrated shade coverage
- Want specialised ninja training features
