Two codes work at Vuly's checkout at the moment, and both apply on top of whatever sale Vuly is running.

## Current Vuly promo codes

| Code | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| BOUNCE15 | A discount on any new Vuly trampoline, swing set or monkey bars |
| BOUNCESURGE | A free gift added to the order |

The codes cover Vuly's current range: the Flare, Ultra 2, Ultra 2 Pro, Thunder 2 and Thunder 2 Pro trampolines, plus the swing sets and monkey bars.

BOUNCE15 is the one that lowers the price. BOUNCESURGE adds a gift and doesn't change the total, so if Vuly's checkout only accepts one code on your order, BOUNCE15 is the one to keep.

## How to apply a code at Vuly's checkout

1. Open the product on [Vuly's site](https://www.vulyplay.com/aff/100/?url=trampoline), choose the size and any accessories, and add it to your cart.
2. Go to the cart and start the checkout.
3. Type the code into the discount code field and apply it.
4. Check the discount or gift shows in the order summary before you pay. If it doesn't, the code hasn't been applied.

The code is applied to the price shown at checkout, so if Vuly has a sale on, the discount comes off the sale price.

## When Vuly runs its own sales

Vuly runs several sales a year, and the [trampoline deals page](/trampoline-deals-sales/) tracks each one. Over the past two years it has recorded a stocktake sale in March, a four-day flash sale in May, a clearance of discontinued models in October, a Black Friday sale through November and a Cyber Monday sale in December. The November and December sales have been the largest, and they usually add free delivery and free accessories such as a ladder, shade cover or basketball set.

Vuly's current offer is on its [promotions page](https://www.vulyplay.com/aff/100/?url=promo).
