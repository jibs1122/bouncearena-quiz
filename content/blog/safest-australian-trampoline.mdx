A report from [CHOICE](https://www.choice.com.au/about-us/media/media-releases/2021/march/new-choice-tests-reveal-safest-trampoline-springfree-r79-scores-90), Australia's leading consumer advocacy group, tested 10 popular trampolines against the Australian safety standard. Only one passed all the safety tests.

## Key findings from CHOICE

CHOICE tested 10 trampoline models against the voluntary Australian standard. Here's what they found:

- Only one model, the Springfree R79, passed all safety tests
- The Jumpflex Flex100 passed main structural tests but had minor safety failures
- Eight other models had major safety failures

## Why the Springfree R79 passed

According to CHOICE testing expert Chris Barnes, the Springfree R79 scored well on:

- Strong, durable frame and enclosure
- Protection from impacts against frame, mat, or enclosure
- No significant entrapment hazards
- Clear and easy-to-follow instructions

## Problems with the other models

The report highlights several issues with the other trampolines tested:

- Serious entrapment hazards
- Padding or enclosures failing durability tests
- Structural failures

## More on Springfree

The detail below is based on our [Springfree Trampoline review](/springfree-trampoline-review).

If Springfree is on your shortlist, you can [view current Springfree prices and models](/go/springfree) before comparing sizes.

### Build quality and materials

- Galvanised, powder-coated steel frame
- 96.6 kg assembled (Medium Round)
- 10-year warranty on the frame, rods, mat and net

### Safety features and standards

Springfree trampolines meet the Australian trampoline standard AS 4989:2015:

- Springless design using fibreglass rods
- Curved safety net poles
- Superfine mesh net

### Bounce and performance

Owner reviews describe the bounce as enjoyable despite the springless design.

Springfree publishes a maximum single-user weight of 100 kg for its full-size models. Its separate rating of up to 700 kg is a static test figure, not a user capacity.

### Ease of assembly

CHOICE praised Springfree's clear and easy-to-follow instructions, but assembly is still a common frustration in owner reviews.

### Value for money

Although Springfree trampolines come with a higher price tag, they offer:

- The safety features above
- A 10-year warranty on frame, springs (fibreglass rods), mat, and safety net

For current pricing, inclusions and offers, [view the latest Springfree range](/go/springfree).

### Accessories

Optional accessories include a shade cover and basketball hoop. A ladder and anchor kit aren't included and cost extra.

### Customer service and warranty

Experiences with Springfree's customer service vary. The 10-year warranty covers all major components.

## Conclusion

Springfree suits families prioritising safety and durability. The drawbacks are difficult assembly and a zip entry rather than a self-closing one.
