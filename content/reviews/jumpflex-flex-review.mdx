The Jumpflex Flex is on [our list of top trampolines for this year](/best-trampolines-australia-2025). It has a powder-coated galvanised steel frame, a tall net and an included ladder at an entry-level price. The drawbacks are 1-year warranties on everything except the frame, and it isn't confirmed to meet the Australian trampoline standard.

## Build quality and materials

The Jumpflex Flex has a galvanised steel frame with a powder-coated finish for weather resistance, which is uncommon on trampolines in this price range.

It uses 48 to 70 springs depending on size (8 ft to 12 ft). Owner reviews suggest the Flex holds up over time and isn't prone to rust or other structural problems.

## Safety features and standards

Safety features include curved safety net poles and a superfine mesh net. The net is tall for this price range and joins to the mat to keep jumpers away from the springs. A ladder is included, which is usually an optional extra.

One downside is that the Flex isn't listed as meeting the voluntary Australian trampoline standard, AS 4989:2015. However, we have been told it meets the international standards ASTM F381-16 and ASTM F2225-15(R2020). In [CHOICE's 2021 test](/safest-australian-trampoline) of ten trampolines against the Australian standard, the Flex passed the main structural tests but had minor safety failures.

## Bounce and performance

The Flex is designed for a maximum of 100 kg per jumper, with a separate 150 kg combined total weight rating. Owner reviews generally describe the bounce as good.

It uses traditional coil springs rather than a springless design, and owners report it works for both small children and adults.

## Ease of assembly

Assembling the Jumpflex Flex is fairly straightforward, and attaching the springs to the mat is the hardest step.

![A Jumpflex Flex trampoline installed in a backyard](/images/posts/jumpflex-flex-1.png)

Replacement parts are available directly from Jumpflex.

## Value for money

Priced from around $750, the Flex is good value for a powder-coated frame, a tall net and an included ladder.

## Additional features and accessories

Optional accessories include a shade cover, slide and basketball hoop. A ladder is included, but the anchor kit is sold separately.

## Customer service

Owner feedback on Jumpflex's customer service is generally positive. Some report delivery delays, but most describe Jumpflex as responsive.

## Warranty

The Flex comes with a 5-year frame warranty and 1-year warranties on the springs, mat, safety net and pads. Longer warranties on the non-frame components would be welcome, but these terms are fairly typical for models in this price range.
