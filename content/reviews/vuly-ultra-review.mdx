If you are buying new, [view Vuly's current range](/go/vuly) and compare the [Ultra 2 with the Ultra 2 Pro](/compare/vuly-ultra-2-vs-ultra-2-pro/).

The Vuly Ultra was Vuly's mid-range coil-spring trampoline, with 70 springs, a 175 cm net and a 150 kg jumper limit. The negatives are a net that Vuly says should be replaced every year, and a ladder and anchor kit that are sold separately.

Vuly's own guide outlines the differences between each model: [Choosing a Vuly Trampoline - Which Model Suits You?](https://www.vulyplay.com/aff/100/?url=blog/choosing-vuly-trampoline)

## Build quality & materials

The Vuly Ultra has 70 springs, each 13.7cm long.

The frame is galvanised steel without the powder coating found on dearer models such as Springfree, the Vuly Thunder Pro and the Lift 2.

Owner reviews occasionally mention problems with the springs and mat, but most describe it as sturdy and weather resistant.

The warning label on the net states that it should be replaced every year.

### Important

The Ultra doesn't come with an anchor kit, so it isn't secured to the ground without the separate [Vuly Trampoline Anchor Kit](https://www.vulyplay.com/aff/100/?url=accessories/anchor-kit).

## Safety features & standards

The Ultra meets the Australian safety standard AS 4989:2015 and has a 175cm safety net with curved poles.

When assembled correctly the mat threads through the base of the net, so fingers can't reach the springs from inside the trampoline.

## Bounce and performance

With a weight capacity of 150kg and 70 springs, the Ultra provides a satisfying bounce for all ages. It employs Vuly’s “Advanced rebound system” which uses two layers of springs to boost momentum based on the bouncer's weight. Owner reviews praise the bounce.

## Ease of assembly

Some owners find the setup straightforward, while others report unclear instructions. [Vuly’s assembly videos](https://www.vulyplay.com/aff/100/?url=installation) help.

## Value for money

At around $1,000 RRP, we think the Ultra is good value for many families given its build, safety features and the 5-year warranty on the frame, springs and mat. The current Ultra 2 starts at around $900.

## Additional features & accessories

The Ultra supports a range of optional accessories, including a shade cover, tent wall and basketball hoop. The shade cover keeps sun and leaves out, and the entry overlaps with no zip to break. Neither a ladder nor an anchor kit is included; both are sold separately.

## Customer service

Owner reviews of Vuly's customer service are mixed, and some report late deliveries around Christmas.

## Warranty

The warranty is 5 years on the frame, springs and mat, and 1 year on the safety net and pads.

## Conclusion

The Vuly Ultra is a solid choice for families wanting a safe, durable spring trampoline. It meets the Australian safety standard, and owners rate the bounce highly.
