These are the two models at the top of Vuly's range, and their published specifications line up almost exactly. Both are round, both meet the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015, both come in M, L and XL, and both carry 10 years on the frame, 5 on the mat and 1 on the net. Weight ratings are effectively the same at around 150 kg per jumper.

The difference is the spring system and the price. The Ultra 2 Pro uses coil springs around the mat. The Thunder 2 Pro uses full-length leaf springs positioned away from the jumping area, and costs several hundred dollars more.

Because every other row matches, this is a straightforward question: is the leaf-spring design worth the price difference to you? If you have not jumped on both, our reviews of each model cover how they feel in use.

Choose the Ultra 2 Pro if you want the same warranty and capacity for less money. Choose the Thunder 2 Pro if the leaf-spring design and its bounce are specifically what you want.

## Quick verdict

The warranty, the standard, sizes and weight ratings all match, so the price gap rests on the spring system alone. The Thunder 2 Pro is worth it only if you want the leaf-spring design, which is a real difference in how the trampoline feels rather than in what it is rated to do.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round, both meet the standard, both are offered in M, L and XL, and they share identical warranty terms. Spring system and price are the only meaningful differences.
