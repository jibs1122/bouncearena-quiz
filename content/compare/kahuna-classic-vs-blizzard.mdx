The Classic and Blizzard are two of Kahuna's coil-spring round ranges, and their specifications are close. Both list a general one-year Australian warranty without separate frame, mat and net terms, and neither is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

The main difference is choice of size. The Classic runs from 6 ft up to 16 ft, while the Blizzard starts at 8 ft and also runs to 16 ft. Both rise from 80 kg at the smaller sizes to 150 kg per jumper at 14 ft and 16 ft.

Prices overlap in the middle of the range. At 8 ft the Blizzard is usually a little cheaper; at 10 ft and 12 ft the Classic often is, though both ranges are frequently discounted so the gap moves.

Choose the Classic if you need the smaller 6 ft size. At shared sizes, compare the current price, spring count and dimensions because the weight ratings and general warranty align closely.

## Quick verdict

There is no clear specification winner at matching sizes, so buy on price, dimensions and availability. The Classic adds a 6 ft entry size; both ranges extend to 14 ft and 16 ft.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round and coil-sprung, with a general one-year warranty rather than separate component terms. The size range, spring counts, dimensions and prices are the rows worth checking.
