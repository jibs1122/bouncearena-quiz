The Classic and Blizzard are Kahuna's two round ranges, and their specifications are close. Both use coil springs, both come with 1-year frame, mat and net warranties, and neither is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

The main difference is choice of size. The Classic runs from 6 ft up to 16 ft, with weight ratings rising from 80 kg to 150 kg per jumper as the sizes increase. The Blizzard covers 8 ft, 10 ft and 12 ft only, and tops out at 125 kg.

Prices overlap in the middle of the range. At 8 ft the Blizzard is usually a little cheaper; at 10 ft and 12 ft the Classic often is, though both ranges are frequently discounted so the gap moves.

Choose the Classic if you want a size above 12 ft or the higher weight rating that comes with it. Choose the Blizzard if it is cheaper in the size you want.

## Quick verdict

There is no meaningful specification advantage either way at matching sizes, so buy on price and availability. If you need 14 ft or 16 ft, the Classic is the only one of the two that goes there.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round, coil-sprung, and carry one year on the frame, mat and net. The size range, spring counts and weight ratings are the rows worth checking.
