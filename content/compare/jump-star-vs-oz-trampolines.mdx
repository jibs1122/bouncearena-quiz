Jump Star and Oz Trampolines are both Australian sellers of budget coil-spring trampolines, and their round models line up closely on size and spring count. The differences are which models are listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015, warranty and shape.

Every current Oz Trampolines model, across the Summit and OzUltimate ranges, is listed as meeting AS 4989:2015. No Jump Star model is listed as meeting the Australian standard. Oz also publishes a 5-year frame warranty and, on most current models, 2 years on the mat and net; Jump Star lists 3 years on the frame and 1 year each on the mat and net.

Jump Star is cheaper at most sizes and sells round models only, up to 16 ft. Oz covers round, oval and rectangular models, with the oval models suiting long narrow yards where a round trampoline wastes space. Both rate their larger models to 150 kg per jumper.

## Quick verdict

Jump Star makes sense when the lowest price is what matters and a round trampoline suits the yard. For a modest step up in price, Oz Trampolines is the better fit for a model that meets the Australian standard, longer published warranties, or an oval or rectangular shape.
