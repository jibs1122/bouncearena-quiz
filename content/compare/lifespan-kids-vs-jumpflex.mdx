Lifespan's springless HyperJump 3 uses elastic straps and is rated to 125 kg at its largest. Jumpflex uses conventional springs and rates the MEGA to 225 kg per jumper and 450 kg combined.

Lifespan Kids sits below Jumpflex across the range. Both brands offer 10-year frame warranties at the top of their ranges, but Lifespan drops to 5 years on some models, and Jumpflex provides mat warranties of 1 to 5 years. Both publish a 1-year net warranty.

Neither brand has a model listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

## Quick verdict

Lifespan Kids suits young children on a budget, or anyone who wants a springless mat. Jumpflex is the better fit if the trampoline needs to carry heavier jumpers, or if you want a bigger mat or a square or rectangular shape.

## Full spec comparison

Lifespan Kids covers round and rectangular in elastic-strap and coil-spring models. Jumpflex covers round in the FLEX and HERO and square or rectangular in the MEGA.
