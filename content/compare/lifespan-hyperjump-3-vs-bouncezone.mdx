These are Lifespan Kids' two main round trampoline ranges. The HyperJump 3 uses elastic straps in place of steel springs, while the BounceZone uses conventional coil springs beneath protective padding.

The HyperJump 3 is available in 8 ft, 10 ft and 12 ft sizes. The BounceZone covers the same sizes and adds a 14 ft model, with higher capacity at the top of the range.

Warranty is a trade-off. HyperJump 3 frames are covered for 10 years but the mat for 1 year. BounceZone frames are covered for 5 years and the mat for 2 years. Neither range in the current table is confirmed against the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

Choose the HyperJump 3 if removing coil springs is the priority. Choose the BounceZone if you want a conventional spring feel, a longer mat warranty or the larger 14 ft size.

## Quick verdict

The HyperJump 3 is the springless choice and carries the longer frame warranty. The BounceZone costs less at the entry size, goes larger and doubles the published mat warranty.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round Lifespan Kids ranges. Spring system, maximum size, frame warranty, mat warranty and top weight rating are the rows that separate them.
