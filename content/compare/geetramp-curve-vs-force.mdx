The Curve and Force are GeeTramp's two main lines, and they differ in shape and scale. Both are listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015, both use coil springs, and both carry a 10-year frame warranty with 3 years on the mat.

The Curve is round, in 10 ft and 12 ft, and is the more affordable of the two. Round mats push jumpers back toward the centre, which suits younger children.

The Force is rectangular, from 7 × 10 ft up to 14 × 16 ft, and costs considerably more. A rectangular mat spreads the bounce more evenly across its surface, which is why the shape is favoured for tricks. The Force is also rated higher, at 130 to 150 kg per jumper against 100 to 130 kg on the Curve. Both above-ground ranges list a 2-year net warranty.

## Quick verdict

The Curve suits a family backyard where a round trampoline at the lower price is enough, and it meets the Australian standard, which is uncommon at its price. The Force makes sense for anyone who wants a rectangle, a higher weight rating, or a larger footprint.
