The Vuly Thunder 2 Pro is round, uses full-length leaf springs set away from the jumping area, and comes in three sizes. The ACON 16 HD is a single 10 × 17 ft rectangle with 140 coil springs, built for a strong, even bounce across the mat.

Both are listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015 and both provide 10-year frame, 5-year mat and 1-year net warranties.

Vuly rates the Thunder 2 Pro to 140 to 150 kg per jumper, while ACON does not publish a single-jumper or combined weight rating for the 16 HD. The ACON also costs considerably more.

## Quick verdict

The Thunder 2 Pro suits anyone who wants a round trampoline with the springs out of the jumping area, in a choice of sizes, for considerably less. The 16 HD is the better fit if you want a large rectangular mat and a 140-spring design, provided you're comfortable that ACON doesn't publish a weight rating for it.
