These are two of the cheapest ways to get a full-size round trampoline with an enclosure into an Australian backyard. Both use coil springs, both run up to 16 ft, and neither is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

Jump Star publishes a 3-year frame warranty and 1-year mat and net warranties. Kahuna lists a general one-year Australian warranty but does not break out separate frame, mat and net terms.

Kahuna has the wider size range, starting at 6 ft where Jump Star starts at 8 ft, and it is usually cheaper at matching sizes. Jump Star is rated to 150 kg per jumper from 12 ft up, where Kahuna reaches 150 kg only at 14 ft and 16 ft.

Choose Jump Star if you want clearly published longer frame coverage or the higher weight rating at mid sizes. Choose the Kahuna Classic if you want the smallest size or the lowest price.

## Quick verdict

Jump Star's published 3-year frame warranty is the main reason to pay a little more, and its weight ratings step up earlier in the range. Kahuna is the cheaper entry and offers a 6 ft size for younger children, but only a general one-year warranty is recorded. Neither is listed as meeting the standard, so if that matters, look at the current Oz Trampolines range at a similar price.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round, coil-sprung, with enclosures and similar spring counts. Check the weight ratings and warranty rows; Jump Star publishes component terms while Kahuna does not.
