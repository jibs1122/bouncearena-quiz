These are two of the cheapest ways to get a full-size round trampoline with an enclosure into an Australian backyard. Both use coil springs, both run up to 16 ft, and neither is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

Jump Star provides a 3-year frame warranty; Kahuna provides 1 year. Both include 1-year mat and net warranties.

Kahuna has the wider size range, starting at 6 ft where Jump Star starts at 8 ft, and it is usually cheaper at matching sizes. Jump Star is rated to 150 kg per jumper from 12 ft up, where Kahuna reaches 150 kg only at 14 ft and 16 ft.

Choose Jump Star if you want the longer frame warranty or the higher weight rating at mid sizes. Choose the Kahuna Classic if you want the smallest size or the lowest price.

## Quick verdict

Jump Star's 3-year frame warranty is the main reason to pay a little more, and its weight ratings step up earlier in the range. Kahuna is the cheaper entry and offers a 6 ft size for younger children. Neither is listed as meeting the standard, so if that matters, look at the Oz Summit range at a similar price.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round, coil-sprung, with enclosures and similar spring counts. The frame warranty row and the weight ratings at each size are the rows to check.
