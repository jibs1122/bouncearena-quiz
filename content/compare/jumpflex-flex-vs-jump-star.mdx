Both of these are budget round trampolines with enclosures, and neither is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

Jump Star is cheaper at matching sizes, goes larger (up to 16 ft against 12 ft on the FLEX) and is rated to 150 kg per jumper at its largest, against a flat 100 kg on the FLEX.

The FLEX comes with a 5-year frame warranty against 3 years from Jump Star, and both have 1-year mat and net warranties.

## Quick verdict

Jump Star suits anyone who wants a bigger trampoline, a higher weight rating, or the lower price. The Jumpflex FLEX makes sense if the longer frame warranty matters more than the extra size.

## Full spec comparison

Both use coil springs.
