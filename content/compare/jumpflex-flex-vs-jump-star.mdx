Both of these are budget round trampolines with enclosures, and neither is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

Jump Star is cheaper at matching sizes, goes larger — up to 16 ft against 12 ft on the FLEX — and is rated to 150 kg per jumper at its largest, against a flat 100 kg on the FLEX. It also comes with a 1-year warranty on both the mat and net.

The FLEX comes with a 5-year frame warranty against 3 years from Jump Star, and both have 1-year mat and net warranties.

Choose Jump Star if you want a bigger trampoline, a higher weight rating, or the lower price. Choose the Jumpflex FLEX if the longer frame warranty is worth more to you than the extra size.

## Quick verdict

For most buyers Jump Star gives more trampoline for less money here, particularly at 14 ft and 16 ft where the FLEX has nothing to offer. The FLEX's advantage is the frame warranty, which is two years longer.
