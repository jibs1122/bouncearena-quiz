The Ultra 2 and Ultra 2 Pro are the same round coil-spring trampoline in most respects. Both meet the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015, both use the same spring system, both stand the same height, and both provide 5-year mat warranties and 1-year net warranties.

Two things change on the Pro. The frame warranty doubles from 5 years to 10, and the weight rating rises from 120 to 130 kg per jumper to a flat 150 kg. The Pro also drops the small S size, so it starts at M.

The frame is the part that cannot be replaced without effectively replacing the trampoline, so the longer frame warranty is the more meaningful of the two changes.

## Quick verdict

The Ultra 2 suits families with primary-school-age children, and anyone who wants the lower price or the smaller S size. The Ultra 2 Pro makes more sense if you expect the trampoline to be handed down or used hard for a decade, because of its 10-year frame warranty. Its 150 kg rating also makes it the better fit if teenagers or adults will use it regularly.
