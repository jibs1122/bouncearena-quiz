The Ultra 2 and Ultra 2 Pro are the same round coil-spring trampoline in most respects. Both meet the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015, both use the same spring system, both stand the same height, and both provide 5-year mat warranties and 1-year net warranties.

Two things change on the Pro. The frame warranty doubles from 5 years to 10, and the weight rating rises from 120 to 130 kg per jumper to a flat 150 kg. The Pro also drops the small S size, so it starts at M.

The frame is the part that cannot be replaced without effectively replacing the trampoline, so the longer frame warranty is the more meaningful of the two changes. The weight rating matters if teenagers or adults will use it regularly.

Choose the Ultra 2 if the trampoline is for younger children and you want the lower price or the smaller S size. Choose the Ultra 2 Pro if you want the 10-year frame warranty or need the higher weight rating.

## Quick verdict

For most families with primary-school-age children, the Ultra 2 does the same job for less. The Pro earns the difference if you expect the trampoline to be handed down, if it will be used hard for a decade, or if older kids and adults will be jumping.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round and coil-sprung, both meet the standard, and they share the same mat and net warranties. The frame warranty, weight rating and available sizes are the rows that differ.
