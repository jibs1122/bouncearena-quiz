Two round trampolines in overlapping sizes, with the ACON costing several hundred dollars more.

Most Air GEN2 sizes meet the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015; the HERO is not listed as meeting it. Both come with a 5-year mat warranty and a 1-year net warranty. Both carry a 10-year frame warranty, and ACON uses more springs at comparable sizes, from 80 up to 110.

The HERO is rated to 160 kg per jumper and 250 kg combined, against 110 kg on the Air GEN2. The HERO is also cheaper at every size, and both top out around 15 ft.

Choose the ACON Air GEN2 if you want a model that meets the standard and the higher spring count. Choose the Jumpflex HERO if the weight rating and price matter more.

## Quick verdict

The Air GEN2 is the only one of the two that meets the standard, and it uses more springs at comparable sizes. The HERO carries considerably more weight for less money, which makes it the more practical choice for households with older or heavier jumpers.
