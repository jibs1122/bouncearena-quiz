Two round trampolines in overlapping sizes, with the ACON costing several hundred dollars more.

The 14 ft and 15 ft Air GEN2 are listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015; the 12 ft is not confirmed, and no HERO size is listed as meeting it. Both carry a 10-year frame warranty, a 5-year mat warranty and a 1-year net warranty. ACON uses more springs at comparable sizes, from 80 up to 110.

The HERO is rated to 160 kg per jumper and 250 kg combined, against 110 kg on the Air GEN2. The HERO is also cheaper at every size, and both top out around 15 ft.

Choose the ACON Air GEN2 at 14 ft or 15 ft if you want a model listed as meeting the standard, and the higher spring count. Choose the Jumpflex HERO if the weight rating and price matter more.

## Quick verdict

The Air GEN2 is the only one of the two listed as meeting the standard, at 14 ft and 15 ft, and it uses more springs at comparable sizes. The HERO carries considerably more weight for less money, which makes it the more practical choice for households with older or heavier jumpers.
