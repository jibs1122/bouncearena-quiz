Vuly and Kahuna are priced very differently. Kahuna is a broad budget range covering several shapes and spring systems. Vuly is a premium range with longer published component warranties, generally higher weight ratings and a larger accessory range.

Warranty is the main difference. Kahuna lists a general one-year Australian warranty but does not break out separate frame, mat and net terms. Vuly publishes a 3-year frame warranty on the Flare and 10 years on the Ultra 2 Pro, Thunder 2 and Thunder 2 Pro, with 5-year mat warranties across most of the range. The frame is the part you cannot easily replace, so clearly stated long coverage matters.

The Australian standard is the other difference. Every Vuly model is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015. No Kahuna model is.

## Quick verdict

Vuly is the only option if the standard matters to you. It also suits anyone who wants the longer warranty, a leaf-spring design that keeps the springs out of the jumping area, or the option to add accessories later. Kahuna suits anyone whose budget is set and who wants the most trampoline for it, and its larger models are rated to the same 150 kg per jumper as Vuly's best.

## Full spec comparison

Vuly's range is round only, across five model families split between coil springs and the leaf-spring Thunder line. Kahuna covers round, oval and rectangular shapes, mostly with coil springs, plus the elastic-band Twister Springless. Kahuna's smaller sizes are rated to 80 kg per jumper against 100 kg on the Vuly Flare.
