Oz Trampolines sits below Vuly on price across the whole range and tops out where Vuly is still mid-range. Both use coil springs on most models, so at similar sizes the bounce is broadly comparable. The differences are warranty, shape and what sits at the top of each range; every current model from both brands is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

Warranty splits by part. Vuly reaches 10 years on the frame for its Ultra 2 Pro and Thunder models, against 5 years across the Oz range. Most Oz models list a 2-year net warranty against 1 year from Vuly. Oz also makes oval and rectangular models, while Vuly is round only. At the top end, Vuly's Thunder 2 and Thunder 2 Pro use leaf springs set away from the jumping area, and nothing in the Oz range is equivalent.

Choose Oz Trampolines if you want a full-size trampoline for less, an oval or rectangular shape, or a longer published net warranty. Choose Vuly if you want a longer frame warranty or the leaf-spring models.

## Quick verdict

For a straightforward backyard trampoline at a lower price, Oz is the value option. Vuly is the step up in frame warranty, weight rating and spring-system choice, and it is the only one of the two with a leaf-spring option.

## Full spec comparison

Oz covers round, oval and rectangular shapes with coil springs across four model families. Vuly is round only across five families, split between coil and leaf springs. Compare the frame and net warranty rows, shapes and spring systems.
