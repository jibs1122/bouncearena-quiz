Springfree's current range runs from around a thousand dollars to well over four thousand, while Oz Trampolines runs from the mid hundreds to around $1,500.

Springfree's construction is the main difference. Flexible fibreglass rods sit beneath the mat instead of springs, and there is no hard frame at the edge of the jumping surface. Oz uses conventional coil springs with padding over them, which is the standard approach and what most Australian backyards already have.

Springfree provides 10-year frame, mat and net warranties. Oz provides 5 years on the frame, with 2-year mat and net warranties listed on most current models. Both brands make oval models for narrow yards; Springfree also makes square ones, while Oz adds rectangles. Oz rates its larger models to 150 kg per jumper against 100 kg on full-size Springfree models, so the cheaper range carries more weight per jumper at the top end.

## Quick verdict

Springfree suits anyone who wants the springless design and 10-year warranties on every part, and is prepared to pay more for them. Oz Trampolines suits anyone who wants a full-size trampoline for a fraction of the price, and every current Summit and OzUltimate model is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.
