These are two round coil-spring trampolines priced a few hundred dollars apart.

Both the Vuly Ultra 2 and OzUltimate Round are listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015. Both provide 5-year frame warranties, but Vuly provides a 5-year mat warranty against 2 years from Oz, while Oz provides a 2-year net warranty against 1 year from Vuly.

The OzUltimate is cheaper at matching sizes and publishes combined weight limits, which Vuly does not. Per-jumper ratings are close: 130 kg on the OzUltimate against 120 to 130 kg on the Ultra 2 depending on size. The Ultra 2 offers a small S size and a slightly larger XL.

## Quick verdict

The Vuly Ultra 2 suits anyone who wants the longer mat warranty or its wider choice of sizes. The OzUltimate is the better fit if you want to spend less, a longer net warranty, or published combined weight limits.
