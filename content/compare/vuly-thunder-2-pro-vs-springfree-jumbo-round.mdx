These are the two brands' flagship round trampolines, and both keep steel springs away from the jumping surface. The Springfree Jumbo Round uses flexible fibreglass rods and has no springs at all. The Thunder 2 Pro uses full-length leaf springs positioned away from the mat.

Both meet the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015 and both provide 10-year frame warranties. Springfree also provides 10-year mat and net warranties, while Vuly provides 5 years on the mat and 1 year on the net.

The Thunder 2 Pro is rated to 140 to 150 kg per jumper against 100 kg on the Jumbo Round, comes in three sizes where the Jumbo Round is a single 4 m model, and its XL is larger than the Springfree. It also costs around a thousand dollars less.

Choose the Springfree Jumbo Round if you want a completely springless design and 10-year warranties on every part. Choose the Vuly Thunder 2 Pro if you want a higher weight rating, a choice of sizes, or to spend less.

## Quick verdict

Springfree's warranty is the most complete on the market and its design removes springs entirely. The Thunder 2 Pro also keeps springs away from the mat, carries more weight, comes in more sizes and costs around a thousand dollars less.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round, both meet the standard, and both carry a 10-year frame warranty. Compare the mat and net warranty rows, which favour Springfree, and the weight, size and price rows, which favour Vuly.
