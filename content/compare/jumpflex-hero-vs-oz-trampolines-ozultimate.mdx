Two round coil-type trampolines at similar sizes, with the OzUltimate consistently cheaper and the HERO better specified.

The HERO is rated to 160 kg per jumper and 250 kg combined, against 130 kg and up to 200 kg on the OzUltimate. Its frame warranty is 10 years against 5. It also runs to 15 ft where the OzUltimate stops at 14 ft.

The HERO comes with a 5-year mat warranty against 2 years on the OzUltimate, though the OzUltimate has a 2-year net warranty against 1 year on the HERO. Neither model is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015, which is worth noting because other Oz Trampolines models — the Summit range — are.

Choose the OzUltimate if you want to spend less and want published mat and net warranties. Choose the HERO if the higher weight rating or the 10-year frame warranty matters more.

## Quick verdict

The HERO is the better trampoline for older kids and heavier jumpers, and its frame warranty is twice as long. The OzUltimate is the value option. If the Australian standard matters to you, note that neither of these has it, and look at the Oz Summit range instead.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round. Both use coil springs, with similar counts at matching sizes. The weight, frame warranty and price rows are where they separate.
