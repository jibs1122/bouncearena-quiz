Two round coil-type trampolines at similar sizes, with the OzUltimate consistently cheaper and the HERO better specified.

The HERO is rated to 160 kg per jumper and 250 kg combined, against 130 kg and up to 200 kg on the OzUltimate. Its frame warranty is 10 years against 5. It also runs to 15 ft where the OzUltimate stops at 14 ft.

The HERO comes with a 5-year mat warranty against 2 years on the OzUltimate, though the OzUltimate has a 2-year net warranty against 1 year on the HERO. The OzUltimate is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015; the HERO is not.

Choose the OzUltimate if you want to spend less and want published mat and net warranties. Choose the HERO if the higher weight rating or the 10-year frame warranty matters more.

## Quick verdict

The HERO is the better trampoline for heavier jumpers, and its frame warranty is twice as long. The OzUltimate is the value option and the only one of these two listed as meeting the Australian standard.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round and use coil springs, with similar counts at matching sizes. The Australian standard, weight, frame warranty and price rows are where they separate.
