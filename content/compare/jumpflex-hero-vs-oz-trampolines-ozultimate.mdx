Both are round coil-spring trampolines at similar sizes, and the OzUltimate is consistently cheaper.

The HERO is rated to 160 kg per jumper and 250 kg combined, against 130 kg and up to 200 kg on the OzUltimate. Its frame warranty is 10 years against 5. It also runs to 15 ft where the OzUltimate stops at 14 ft.

The HERO comes with a 5-year mat warranty against 2 years on the OzUltimate, though the OzUltimate has a 2-year net warranty against 1 year on the HERO. The OzUltimate is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015; the HERO is not.

## Quick verdict

The OzUltimate suits anyone who wants a model listed as meeting the Australian standard, a lower price or the longer net warranty. The HERO is better suited to heavier jumpers because it is rated to 160 kg per jumper, and its frame warranty is twice as long.

Spring counts are similar at matching sizes.
