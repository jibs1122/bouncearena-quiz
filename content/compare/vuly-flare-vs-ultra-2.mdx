The Flare is Vuly's entry model and the Ultra 2 sits a step above it. Both are round, both use coil springs, and both meet the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

The Ultra 2 has longer warranties and a higher weight rating. Its frame and mat warranties are 5 years against 3 on the Flare, and it is rated to 120 to 130 kg per jumper against 100 kg. It also comes in four sizes from S to XL, where the Flare offers two, and its XL is noticeably larger than the biggest Flare.

The Flare's advantage is price: it starts at roughly half what the Ultra 2 does, which makes it one of the cheaper trampolines in Australia that meets the standard.

## Quick verdict

The Flare suits a tight budget and younger children. The Ultra 2 is the better fit for older children because of its higher weight rating, and for anyone who wants the longer warranties or a larger size.
