Both are springless round trampolines, with Springfree priced well above the Lifespan range.

The Springfree Medium Round uses flexible fibreglass rods beneath the mat. The Lifespan HyperJump 3 uses elastic straps. Both remove steel springs from beside the jumper, but neither the construction nor the warranties are comparable.

Springfree provides 10-year frame, mat and net warranties and meets the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015. The HyperJump 3 matches the 10-year frame warranty but provides only 1 year on the mat and net, and is not listed as meeting the Australian standard.

Size and capacity favour the cheaper model. The HyperJump 3 runs to 12 ft and is rated to 125 kg per jumper at that size, where the Springfree Medium Round is a single 3 m model rated to 100 kg.

Choose Springfree if you want a springless design that meets the standard, with a decade-long warranty on every part. Choose the Lifespan HyperJump 3 if you want a springless mat, a larger jumping area and a much lower price.

## Quick verdict

On size and weight for the money, the HyperJump 3 is well ahead. Springfree meets the standard, uses the rod system, and has the longest warranty of any brand here. Which matters more depends on whether you are buying for a decade or for the next few years.
