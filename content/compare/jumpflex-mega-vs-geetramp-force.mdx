Both of these are built for harder use than a standard backyard round trampoline, and both carry a 10-year frame warranty.

The MEGA has the higher capacity by a clear margin: 225 kg per jumper and 450 kg combined, against 130 to 150 kg on the Force. It also comes in square as well as rectangular, at 14 ft and 19 ft square and 17 ft rectangular.

The Force meets the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015 across every size and the MEGA is not listed as meeting it. GeeTramp provides a 2-year net warranty against 1 year on the MEGA, though the MEGA provides a 5-year mat warranty against 3 years on the Force. The Force comes in five sizes from 7 × 10 ft up to 14 × 16 ft, starts at around half the MEGA's entry price, and is available as an in-ground model.

Choose the GeeTramp Force if you want a model that meets the standard, more size choices, an in-ground option or a lower price. Choose the Jumpflex MEGA if the weight rating is the deciding factor or you want a square mat.

## Quick verdict

The Force covers more buyers: it meets the standard, costs less, and comes in more sizes and in-ground. The MEGA suits households where the load matters most, particularly adults or several jumpers at once, and it is the only one of the two with a square mat.
