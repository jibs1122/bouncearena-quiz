Springfree removes springs entirely: flexible fibreglass rods sit beneath the mat, outside the jumping surface. The Vuly Thunder 2 keeps springs but uses leaf springs mounted away from the mat edge. Both meet the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015 and both are round.

Warranty favours Springfree, which provides 10-year frame, mat and net warranties. The Thunder 2 provides 10 years on the frame, 5 on the mat and 1 on the net. The Thunder 2 is rated to 140 to 150 kg per jumper, against 100 kg on full-size Springfree round models.

Price is the other gap. The Thunder 2 starts well below Springfree's round range, and its XXL size is larger than Springfree's Jumbo Round.

Springfree suits anyone who wants no springs at all and long warranties on every part. The Vuly Thunder 2 is the better fit if you want a higher weight rating, a larger maximum size, or a lower price.

## Quick verdict

Both solve the same problem and both meet the Australian standard. Springfree removes springs entirely and is warranted for a decade on every part. The Thunder 2 keeps springs but moves them away from the mat, costs noticeably less and carries more weight, which suits households with older kids.
