The main differences between Jumpflex and Oz Trampolines are the safety standard and capacity. Every current Oz Summit and OzUltimate model is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015, while no Jumpflex model is. However, Jumpflex has much higher weight ratings, with the MEGA rated to 225 kg per jumper and 450 kg combined, and the HERO to 160 kg, against 150 kg at the top of the Oz range.

Warranty favours Jumpflex on the frame, at 10 years on the HERO and MEGA against 5 years across the Oz range. The HERO and MEGA also come with 5-year mat warranties, while most Oz models list 2 years on the mat and net against a 1-year net warranty from Jumpflex. Oz is cheaper throughout and offers oval and rectangular models; Jumpflex adds square models in the MEGA line and goes considerably higher at the top of its range.

## Quick verdict

Oz Trampolines suits a family that wants a model listed as meeting the Australian standard at a lower price, or anyone who wants an oval shape. Jumpflex is the better fit for heavier or older jumpers because of its much higher weight ratings. It also suits anyone who wants the longer frame warranty or a square mat, provided you're comfortable that no Jumpflex model is listed as meeting the standard.

## Full spec comparison

Jumpflex uses coil springs on the round FLEX and HERO and piano wire springs on the MEGA. Oz uses coil springs throughout.
