The HyperJump 3 is springless, using elastic straps rather than steel springs, which removes the exposed spring beside the jumper. The Kahuna Classic uses conventional coil springs under padding. Neither is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

The HyperJump 3 publishes a 10-year frame warranty and 1-year mat and net warranties. Kahuna lists a general one-year Australian warranty but does not break out separate frame, mat and net terms.

Kahuna is cheaper at most sizes, goes to 16 ft where the HyperJump 3 stops at 12 ft, and is rated to 150 kg per jumper at its largest against 125 kg.

## Quick verdict

The Lifespan HyperJump 3 suits anyone who wants a springless mat and a 10-year frame warranty, for a modest step up in price. The Kahuna Classic is the better fit for the largest possible trampoline for the least money.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round with enclosures.
