Both brands sit at the budget end and offer both sprung and springless choices. Kahuna is mostly coil-sprung across round, oval and rectangular shapes, with the elastic-band Twister Springless. Lifespan Kids sells the springless HyperJump 3, which also uses elastic straps instead of steel springs, alongside conventional coil-spring models.

Warranty detail is where they separate. Lifespan Kids provides a 10-year frame warranty on the HyperJump 3 and 5 years on the other current models. Kahuna lists a general one-year Australian warranty but does not break out separate frame, mat and net terms. Check the component rows against the exact model you want.

No model from either brand is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015. If that standard is a requirement, this is not the right pair to choose between.

Choose Lifespan Kids if you want a springless mat with a published 10-year frame warranty. Choose Kahuna if you want the broadest choice of low-priced sizes and shapes.

## Quick verdict

Lifespan Kids publishes longer component warranties, while Kahuna undercuts it on price and offers more shapes. Both have an elastic-band springless option. Read the frame warranty row per model on the Lifespan side, because it is not consistent; Kahuna does not publish a separate frame term.

## Full spec comparison

Kahuna covers round, oval and rectangular shapes, mostly with coil springs, plus the springless Twister. Lifespan Kids covers round and rectangular, split between elastic-strap springless models and coil-spring models. Frame warranties vary within the Lifespan range, so read that row per model.
