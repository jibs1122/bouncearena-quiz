Both brands sit at the budget end, but they offer different things for the money. Kahuna is coil springs throughout, in round and one oval. Lifespan Kids sells the springless HyperJump 3, which uses elastic straps instead of steel springs, alongside conventional coil-spring models.

Warranty is where they separate. Lifespan Kids provides a 10-year frame warranty on the HyperJump 3 and 5 years on the other current models. Kahuna provides 1-year frame, mat and net warranties across the range. Neither publishes an especially long mat warranty, so check that row against the model you want.

No model from either brand in the current table is confirmed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015. If that standard is a requirement, this is not the right pair to choose between.

Choose Lifespan Kids if you want a springless mat at a budget price or a 10-year frame warranty. Choose Kahuna if you want the largest trampoline for the least money.

## Quick verdict

Lifespan Kids offers more for the money in warranty terms and is the only one of the two with a springless option. Kahuna undercuts it on price and goes larger at the top of its range. Read the frame warranty row per model on the Lifespan side, because it is not consistent.

## Full spec comparison

Kahuna is coil-sprung in round and oval. Lifespan Kids covers round and rectangular, split between elastic-strap springless models and coil-spring models. Frame warranties vary within the Lifespan range; AS 4989 is not confirmed for either range in the current table.
