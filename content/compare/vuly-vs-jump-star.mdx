Both brands sell round trampolines with enclosures, and both rate their largest models to 150 kg per jumper. Beyond that they are aimed at different budgets and back their products very differently.

Every Vuly model is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015. No Jump Star model is. Vuly provides frame warranties of up to 10 years on the Ultra 2 Pro and Thunder models and mat warranties of 3 to 5 years; Jump Star provides a 3-year frame warranty and 1-year mat and net warranties.

Jump Star is the cheaper brand at every size and does one thing: round trampolines with enclosures from 8 ft to 16 ft. Vuly's range runs from the entry-level Flare up to the leaf-spring Thunder 2 Pro, with accessories and a much higher ceiling on price.

Choose Vuly if you want a model that meets the standard, a longer warranty or the leaf-spring models. Choose Jump Star if you want a straightforward round trampoline at the lowest price.

## Quick verdict

Vuly is the more premium range in every measurable way here: every model meets the standard, warranties are longer, and there is more choice. Jump Star is a straightforward budget buy, and its 3-year frame warranty is better than some rivals at the same price, but nothing in the range is listed as meeting it.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round with enclosures. Vuly splits between coil and leaf springs across five families; Jump Star is coil-sprung in a single family across five sizes. The Australian standard and warranty rows are where the price difference shows up.
