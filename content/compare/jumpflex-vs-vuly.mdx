Jumpflex and Vuly are similarly priced, but there are some clear differences between them. Every Vuly model is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015, while no Jumpflex model is.

However, Jumpflex has much higher weight limits, with the HERO rated to 160 kg per jumper, and the MEGA to 225 kg per jumper and 450 kg combined. Vuly's range runs from 100 to 150 kg. Jumpflex also makes square and rectangular models, while Vuly is round only.

Warranty terms are closer. Both brands offer 10 years on the frame at the top of their ranges. Vuly covers the mat for 3 to 5 years and Jumpflex for 1 to 5 years, depending on the model, and both give the net a 1-year warranty.

Vuly starts cheaper, and its Thunder models use leaf springs positioned away from the jumping area. Jumpflex doesn't offer an equivalent design.

## Quick verdict

Vuly makes more sense if the Australian standard matters, or if you want a leaf-spring trampoline or a longer mat warranty on one of the cheaper models. Jumpflex is better suited to older kids and adults because of its higher weight ratings. It also suits anyone who wants a square or rectangular mat or more size for the money, provided you're comfortable that no Jumpflex model is listed as meeting the standard.

## Full spec comparison

Jumpflex uses coil springs on the round FLEX and HERO and piano wire springs on the square and rectangular MEGA. Vuly uses coil springs on the Flare, Ultra 2 and Ultra 2 Pro, and leaf springs on the Thunder 2 and Thunder 2 Pro.
