Springfree removes springs entirely, using flexible fibreglass rods under the mat, and every model is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015. Jumpflex uses conventional springs, with higher weight ratings and lower prices.

The capacity gap is large. Jumpflex rates its MEGA models to 225 kg per jumper and 450 kg combined, and the HERO to 160 kg. Full-size Springfree models are rated to 100 kg per jumper.

No Jumpflex model is listed as meeting AS 4989:2015. Jumpflex provides 10-year frame warranties on the HERO and MEGA and 5 years on the FLEX, with 5 years on the mat and 1 on the net, while Springfree provides 10-year warranties on all three parts.

## Quick verdict

Springfree suits anyone who wants a springless design that meets the standard, with one long warranty on every part. Jumpflex is better suited to teenagers and adults because of its much higher weight ratings. It also suits anyone who wants a square or rectangular mat or a considerably lower price, provided you're comfortable with far shorter mat and net warranties and no model listed as meeting the standard.

## Full spec comparison

Springfree comes in round, oval and square. Jumpflex covers round in the FLEX and HERO and square or rectangular in the MEGA, using coil springs on the round models and piano wire springs on the MEGA.
