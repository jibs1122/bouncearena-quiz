Both are round coil-spring trampolines with a 10-year frame warranty, and they are within a couple of hundred dollars of each other.

The main differences are the Australian standard listing and weight limits. The Ultra 2 Pro is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015 and the HERO is not. However, the HERO is rated to 160 kg per jumper and 250 kg combined, against 150 kg on the Ultra 2 Pro. Both have a 5-year mat warranty and a 1-year net warranty.

Sizes are comparable, with the HERO reaching 15 ft and the Ultra 2 Pro's XL close behind. The HERO is the cheaper of the two.

## Quick verdict

The Ultra 2 Pro suits anyone who wants a model listed as meeting the Australian standard. The HERO is the better fit for older or heavier jumpers, with its 160 kg per-jumper rating, and for a lower price.
