The Jumpflex HERO has a higher weight limit and a longer frame warranty, while the Vuly Ultra 2 is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015 and the HERO is not.

The HERO is rated to 160 kg per jumper and 250 kg combined, against 120 to 130 kg on the Ultra 2, and its frame warranty is 10 years against 5. It also runs up to 15 ft, where the Ultra 2's XL is a little smaller.

Both come with 5-year mat warranties and 1-year net warranties. The Ultra 2 offers a small S size for tight yards, which the HERO does not.

## Quick verdict

The HERO is better suited to older or heavier jumpers because its single-jumper limit is 160 kg, and it has the longer frame warranty. The Ultra 2 makes more sense if meeting the Australian standard matters, or if the yard needs the smaller S size.

## Full spec comparison

Both are round and use coil springs.
