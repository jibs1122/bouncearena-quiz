Both of these are round trampolines with enclosures at the budget end, and they split on whether they meet the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015.

The Vuly Flare is listed as meeting AS 4989:2015. No Jump Star model is. Both provide 3-year frame warranties, but Vuly provides a 3-year mat warranty against 1 year from Jump Star.

Jump Star has the range. It runs from 8 ft to 16 ft where the Flare offers two sizes topping out around 12.7 ft, and its larger models are rated to 150 kg per jumper against a flat 100 kg on the Flare. Jump Star's smaller sizes undercut the Flare on price, though its largest models cost more than the Flare does.

Choose the Vuly Flare if you want a model that meets the standard and a longer mat warranty on a smaller trampoline. Choose Jump Star if you need a larger size or a higher weight rating.

## Quick verdict

The Flare is the only one of the two that meets the standard, which matters if the Australian standard is on your list. Jump Star is the only one of the two that comes in 14 ft and 16 ft.
