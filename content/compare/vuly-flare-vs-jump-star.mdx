Both are round trampolines with enclosures at the budget end. The Vuly Flare is listed as meeting AS 4989:2015, while no Jump Star model is. Both provide 3-year frame warranties, but Vuly provides a 3-year mat warranty against 1 year from Jump Star.

Jump Star has the wider size range. It runs from 8 ft to 16 ft where the Flare offers two sizes topping out around 12.7 ft, and its larger models are rated to 150 kg per jumper against a flat 100 kg on the Flare. Jump Star's smaller sizes undercut the Flare on price, though its largest models cost more than the Flare does.

## Quick verdict

The Vuly Flare suits anyone who wants a model listed as meeting the standard and a longer mat warranty on a smaller trampoline. Jump Star is the better fit if you need a 14 ft or 16 ft trampoline, which the Flare doesn't come in, or the higher weight rating on its larger models.

## Full spec comparison

Both use coil springs.
