Both are round coil-spring trampolines with enclosures, covering 6 ft up to 16 ft, at prices within a couple of hundred dollars of each other.

Every Summit Round size is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015. No Kahuna Classic size is. The Summit publishes a 5-year frame warranty and a 2-year net warranty, with a 2-year mat warranty recorded on several sizes. Kahuna lists a general one-year Australian warranty but does not break out separate component terms. The Summit also publishes combined weight limits, which Kahuna does not.

Kahuna is the cheaper of the two at matching sizes, and its largest models are rated to 150 kg per jumper against 110 to 130 kg on the Summit.

## Quick verdict

The Oz Summit suits families who want a model listed as meeting the Australian standard and clearly published component warranties. The Kahuna Classic is the better fit if the lowest price is the deciding factor or you need the higher weight rating at 14 ft and 16 ft.

Both have comparable spring counts.
