Springfree and BERG are both premium brands, priced in the same broad range. Springfree is springless, using flexible fibreglass rods, and every model is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015. BERG uses its TwinSpring system, a conventional sprung design, and no BERG model is listed as meeting the standard.

Warranty splits by part. BERG provides a frame warranty of up to 13 years, longer than any other brand here, against 10 years from Springfree. Springfree then provides 10-year mat and net warranties, where BERG provides 2 years on each.

BERG rates its models to 120 kg per jumper against 100 kg on full-size Springfree models, and offers in-ground Champion versions. Springfree comes in round, oval and square, and goes considerably larger at the top of its range than BERG does.

## Quick verdict

Springfree suits anyone who wants the springless design, the standard and long warranties on every part. BERG is the better fit if you want an in-ground install or the longest frame warranty, provided you're comfortable with 2-year mat and net warranties and no model listed as meeting the standard.

BERG makes round and rectangular models.
