Both brands sell springless trampolines, and the price difference between them is large. Springfree uses flexible fibreglass rods beneath the mat. Lifespan Kids' HyperJump 3 uses elastic straps. Both remove steel springs from the edge of the jumping surface, but they are not the same construction and they are not priced anywhere near each other.

Springfree meets the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015 across the whole range and provides 10-year frame, mat and net warranties. No Lifespan model in the current table is confirmed against AS 4989. Lifespan's frame warranty reaches 10 years on the HyperJump 3, with 5 years on the BounceZone models and Hoppy. Mat cover runs 1 to 2 years, with 1 year on the net.

Lifespan rates its largest springless model to 125 kg per jumper against 100 kg on full-size Springfree models, so the cheaper option actually carries more weight. Springfree offers round, oval and square and goes much larger at the top of its range.

Choose Springfree if you want a springless design that meets the standard, with long warranties on every part. Choose Lifespan Kids if you want a springless mat for a fraction of the price and can live without the standard.

## Quick verdict

Springfree costs more because every model meets the standard and the warranty runs for 10 years on every part. Lifespan Kids is the budget route to a springless mat and carries a higher per-jumper rating, but the standard and warranty terms are not comparable.

## Full spec comparison

Springfree is springless in round, oval and square, meeting the standard throughout, with 10-year warranties on every part. Lifespan Kids covers round and rectangular across elastic-strap and coil models with varying terms. Read the Australian standard and frame warranty rows per Lifespan model.
