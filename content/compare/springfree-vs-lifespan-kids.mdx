Both brands sell springless trampolines, and the price difference between them is large. Springfree uses flexible fibreglass rods beneath the mat, while Lifespan Kids' HyperJump 3 uses elastic straps.

Springfree meets the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015 across the whole range and provides 10-year frame, mat and net warranties. No Lifespan model is listed as meeting AS 4989:2015. Lifespan's frame warranty reaches 10 years on the HyperJump 3, with 5 years on the BounceZone models and Hoppy. Mat cover runs 1 to 2 years, with 1 year on the net.

Lifespan rates its largest springless model to 125 kg per jumper against 100 kg on full-size Springfree models, so the cheaper option carries more weight. Springfree offers round, oval and square and goes much larger at the top of its range.

## Quick verdict

Springfree suits anyone who wants a springless design that meets the standard, with 10-year warranties on every part. Lifespan Kids is the better fit if you want a springless mat for a fraction of the price, or a higher per-jumper rating, and can live without the standard and Springfree's longer mat and net warranties.

## Full spec comparison

Lifespan Kids makes round and rectangular models, with elastic straps or coil springs depending on the model.
