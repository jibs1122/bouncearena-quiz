Both of these keep steel springs away from the jumper. The Thunder 2 uses leaf springs mounted away from the mat edge, while the HyperJump 3 uses elastic straps in place of springs.

The Thunder 2 costs roughly double or more. It is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015; the HyperJump 3 is not. It also comes with a 5-year mat warranty against 1 year. Both provide a 10-year frame warranty and a 1-year net warranty. The Thunder 2 is rated to 140 to 150 kg per jumper against 100 to 125 kg and goes larger than the HyperJump 3's 12 ft maximum.

## Quick verdict

The Thunder 2 suits anyone who wants a model listed as meeting the standard, longer warranties on the wear parts and a larger size. The HyperJump 3 is the better fit if you want a springless mat at a fraction of the price and can accept the shorter warranties, though its frame warranty is as long as Vuly's.
