BERG starts at roughly the price of Vuly's top model, so these two only really overlap at the premium end.

The Australian standard is the first difference. Every Vuly model is listed as meeting the Australian Trampoline Standard AS 4989:2015. No BERG model is. BERG's frame warranty is the longest of any brand here, at 13 years on the Champion, against 10 years on Vuly's best, though BERG provides 2-year mat and net warranties, where Vuly provides 3 to 5 years on the mat and 1 year on the net.

BERG makes round and rectangular models, including in-ground Champion versions that sit flush with the lawn. Vuly is round only and above ground. Vuly rates its Ultra 2 Pro and Thunder models to 150 kg per jumper against 120 kg on BERG, and its Thunder models use leaf springs positioned away from the jumping area.

## Quick verdict

Vuly suits anyone who wants a model that meets the standard, a higher weight rating or a lower price. BERG is the better fit if you want an in-ground install, a rectangle, or the 13-year frame warranty on the Champion.

## Full spec comparison

Vuly's range is split between coil and leaf springs, and BERG uses its TwinSpring system throughout.
